One Roof, From Factory to Port

Manufacturing and export under one roof

Our own fully licensed factories handle every stage of production, and we make sure all required export paperwork is in order before any shipment leaves. Our experienced team carefully sorts out impurities and packs each order into our own bags, and over the years we've built lasting export relationships with clients across the Gulf, the Middle East, Europe and the Americas.

  • Private factories, officially licensed for export
  • Precise sorting plus professional packaging — we can even print your company's name on the bag
  • Fixed, regular shipping schedules to ports worldwide
  • Double quality inspection, before and after loading, on every shipment
