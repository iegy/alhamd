Our Story

A family legacy that began with a real passion for charcoal

Al-Hamd's journey began more than thirty years ago, from a simple belief: Egyptian natural charcoal deserves to reach tables around the world at the highest quality standard. Generation after generation, our expertise grew from local production into global export, never compromising on the standards that built our name.

Today, we're proud to export the finest natural and premium African charcoal to more than 19 countries worldwide, with full control over every stage of production — from selecting the raw material to the shipment reaching our client's door — through regular land and sea shipping and flexible packaging for every order size.

Step by Step

How a piece of charcoal reaches our client

01

Selecting & Preparing Wood

We select the right wood species for each grade — citrus, casuarina, acacia, guava, mango — for distinct ignition and aroma in every product.

02

Production & Carbonization

The wood goes through careful production stages, overseen by a team with deep experience spanning more than three decades.

03

Sorting & Inspection

Every piece passes under our team's eyes to remove impurities and broken pieces, keeping only first-grade charcoal.

04

Packaging to Order

We pack charcoal in sizes from 1 kg up to 25 kg, matched to each client's needs and target market.

05

Shipping & Delivery

Regular land and sea shipping to 19+ countries, with direct follow-up on every shipment until it arrives safely.
